01 / Read the business

Three changes behind the headline.

51.7%

Services' share of revenue growth

Services added $13.0B of the $25.1B increase in FY2025 sales. Its revenue share reached 26.2%, but it contributed 42.2% of gross profit. Mix matters as well as total sales.

7.7% vs 19.5%

Two ways to read profit growth

FY2025 net income rose 19.5% against reported FY2024. Against Apple's FY2024 adjusted net income, excluding a $10.246B tax charge, growth was 7.7%. The adjusted comparison is supplemental, not a replacement for GAAP.

−5.7%

Cash flow moved the other way

Operating cash flow fell to $111.5B while net income rose to $112.0B. Higher capital spending also reduced CFO less capex to $98.8B. Cash taxes paid rose from $26.1B to $43.4B; the cash-flow reconciliation needs separate attention.

Calculated from Apple's official earnings statements, sources S25 and S24. Figures rounded; see the source data for exact amounts.

A changing revenue mix

ProductsServices

The analytical question

Services' FY2025 gross margin was 75.4%, compared with 36.8% for products. Faster services growth can lift consolidated margins even if each business's margin stays flat.

That is a hypothesis about future economics, not a guarantee. Product demand supports the ecosystem; the two revenue streams are not independent. This model makes the mix effect visible, without claiming to model that feedback loop.

How to interpret this case

Evaluate the operating story before choosing a discount rate. Does the service growth assumption require a larger device base, higher spending per user, or both? What would prevent the assumed margin from persisting?

A large terminal contribution means the five-year forecast explains only part of the result. The perpetual growth and reinvestment assumptions deserve as much scrutiny as near-term earnings.

No current stock price is included, so the tool makes no claim that Apple is under- or overvalued today.

04 / Model notes

Every step can be followed.

  1. Revenue and operating profit. Grow products and services separately from FY2025 actuals. Apply separate gross margins, then subtract operating expenses as a share of combined revenue.
  2. Unlevered cash flow. NOPAT = EBIT × (1 − tax rate). FCFF = NOPAT + depreciation and amortization − capex − increase in operating net working capital. Discount the five annual FCFF values at year-end using WACC.
  3. Terminal reinvestment. Terminal NOPAT = FY2030 NOPAT × (1 + g). Terminal FCFF = terminal NOPAT × (1 − g / terminal ROIC). Terminal value = terminal FCFF / (WACC − g), discounted five years. This normalizes reinvestment instead of perpetuating the low explicit-period net investment ratio.
  4. Equity bridge. Add cash plus current and non-current marketable securities of $132.420B; subtract commercial paper and current/non-current term debt of $98.657B. Net adjustment: +$33.763B. These are fiscal-year-end book amounts, not updated market values.

Scope, accounting choices & limitations
  • The source snapshots are Apple's official unaudited earnings-release statements, manually transcribed and checked through statement identities. This release does not claim an automated SEC ingestion pipeline.
  • The model uses a fiscal-year-end reference date with information released afterward. It is a retrospective teaching case, not an investable point-in-time backtest. Subsequent FY2026 disclosures are excluded.
  • All cash and marketable securities are treated as non-operating assets available to equity. Required operating cash, taxes on liquidation, and other valuation adjustments are not modeled.
  • Operating lease costs remain in operating expenses; lease liabilities are not separately treated as financial debt. No full lease recast is performed.
  • Stock-based compensation stays in operating expenses and is not added back. Future share-count changes and dilution are omitted.
  • The working-capital assumption is deliberately simplified. It does not reconstruct Apple's full balance sheet, supplier financing, tax timing or the cash-flow statement's operating adjustments.
  • Terminal ROIC is assumed rather than estimated; there is an immediate transition to normalized reinvestment after FY2030. Scenario ranges are not confidence intervals.
  • This is a standalone educational valuation case, not a complete three-statement model or investment recommendation.

05 / Reproduce the work

Source numbers. Open calculations.

Download model.py and data.json into the same folder, then run python model.py. Python 3 standard library only. The script checks historical statement identities and exports the baseline forecast, historical data and valuation results.
